Privacy Policy

Bulkify · Last updated: May 21, 2026

Bulkify is a fitness tracking application built for personal use. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how. The short version: Bulkify does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on any server. Everything stays on your device.

Information We Collect

None on our servers. Bulkify does not have a backend, accounts, or login system. All information you enter — including weight, height, age, sex, fitness goal, food logs, workout history, and weight check-ins — is stored locally on your device using your phone's built-in secure storage (AsyncStorage on Android).

This data never leaves your device through Bulkify.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or analytics
  • Photos, contacts, or any other phone permissions
  • Payment or financial information
  • Health data from Apple Health, Google Fit, or similar services

Third-Party Services

Bulkify loads workout illustration images from Unsplash, a free image hosting service. When these images load, Unsplash's servers receive a standard web request that includes your device's IP address — this is how the internet works for any image loaded from a website. We do not send any other information about you to Unsplash. You can review Unsplash's privacy practices at unsplash.com/privacy.

Bulkify does not use any analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.), advertising networks, or third-party SDKs that collect data.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because all your data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it:

  • To delete specific entries: use the delete option within the app
  • To clear all data: use the "Reset" option in the Profile screen, which wipes all your logged data and onboarding info
  • To remove everything permanently: uninstall the Bulkify app — this removes all stored data along with the app

Children's Privacy

Bulkify is not directed at children under the age of 13. Because the app does not collect any personal information, there is no information about children that could be collected, stored, or shared. The app is recommended for users aged 16 and above due to its fitness and nutrition tracking nature.

Changes to This Policy

If Bulkify ever adds features that involve collecting data (such as cloud sync, accounts, or analytics), this policy will be updated before those features are released. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after a policy update means you accept the revised terms.
